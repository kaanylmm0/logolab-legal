# LogoLab Support

Welcome to the LogoLab support page.

LogoLab is an AI-powered logo creation app that helps users generate logo ideas based on their preferences, styles, colors, industries, and custom instructions.

## Contact Support

## Account and Sign-In Issues

If you are having trouble signing in, registering, or accessing your account, please contact us using the email address above.

## Subscription and Purchases

LogoLab Pro subscriptions are processed through the Apple App Store.

If you need to manage or cancel your subscription, you can do so from your Apple ID subscription settings.

For purchase-related questions, you can also contact us by email.

## Logo Generation Issues

If a logo generation fails or does not complete correctly, please:

1. Check your internet connection.
2. Close and reopen the app.
3. Try generating the logo again.
4. Contact us if the problem continues.

## Saving Logos

LogoLab may request permission to save generated logos to your photo library.

If saving does not work, please check that photo access is enabled for LogoLab in your device settings.
