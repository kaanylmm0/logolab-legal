## 1. About LogoLab

LogoLab is an AI-powered logo generation application that allows users to create logo concepts using artificial intelligence.

Depending on the features available in the application, users may:

* Create an account or sign in using supported authentication methods.
* Generate logos using AI.
* Select logo styles, colors, categories, or other generation options.
* Save generated logos to the application or device.
* Save images to the device photo library or gallery.
* Access free or paid generation plans.
* Purchase and manage subscriptions.
* Restore previous purchases.
* View advertisements or rewarded advertisements.
* Delete their LogoLab account.

LogoLab uses third-party services to provide some of these features.

## 3. AI Logo Generation Information

When you request a logo, LogoLab may process information that you provide for the purpose of generating the requested image.

This may include:

* Logo prompt.
* Business or brand name.
* Logo description.
* Style selections.
* Color selections.
* Industry or category selections.
* Design preferences.
* Other options selected during generation.

This information may be transmitted to an artificial intelligence service provider such as **Google Gemini** in order to generate the requested logo.

AI generation requests are processed only as necessary to provide the requested functionality and according to the terms and privacy practices of the applicable AI service provider.

You should not include confidential information, passwords, financial information, sensitive personal data, private documents, or third-party personal information in your logo prompts.

## 4. Generated Images

LogoLab allows users to generate images using artificial intelligence.

Generated images may be temporarily processed by the application's technical infrastructure or third-party AI provider as necessary to complete the generation request.

Logos that you intentionally save in the **My Logos** section may be stored locally on your device unless otherwise indicated within the application.

Images that you export or save to your photo library are stored on your device and are subject to your device's own storage, synchronization, cloud backup, and photo library settings.

LogoLab does not guarantee that locally stored images will remain available after:

* Uninstalling the application.
* Clearing application data.
* Resetting the device.
* Removing the files manually.
* Changing devices without transferring application data.
